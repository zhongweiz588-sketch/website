FAQ

Got questions?

What services do we offer?

We provide full-service interior design, home styling, space planning, and renovation support for residential and select commercial projects.

How does a typical project start?

With a consultation and site review to understand your space, goals, and lifestyle, then we define scope and a clear design direction.

Do you handle renovations as well as design?

Yes. We offer end-to-end renovation support, aligning layout improvements and finishes with your long-term vision.

Can you work with an existing space or furniture?

Absolutely. We frequently design around existing pieces, integrating them into a cohesive, refreshed layout.

What is the typical project timeline?

Timelines vary by scope, but most residential projects run 6–12 weeks from concept to delivery.
